CP-Titanium Micro-Milling

An experimental manufacturing study investigating burr formation and dimensional accuracy during micro-machining of a CP-titanium implant geometry.

Role
Research project
Focus
Micro-machining
Status
Completed
Tools
CAD/CAM, CNC, Metrology, ImageJ
MANUFACTURING RESEARCH

Problem

Micro-machining titanium requires careful parameter control because small changes can affect burr height and dimensional accuracy. The study treated machining quality as a measurable engineering problem rather than a purely visual result.

Approach

  • Prepared the implant geometry through CAD/CAM workflow.
  • Used CNC micro-milling to machine CP-titanium samples.
  • Measured burr formation and dimensional accuracy through metrology and image analysis.
  • Interpreted experimental results against machining parameter choices.

Outcome

This project demonstrates research discipline, manufacturing awareness, and the ability to connect CAD/CAM preparation with physical machining results and measurement-backed analysis.
