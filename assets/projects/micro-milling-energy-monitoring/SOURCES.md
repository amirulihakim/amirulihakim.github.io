## Source limitations

- The PCB machining photograph is only 208 x 365 pixels in the report; the PCB schematic is 316 x 280. Their source resolution has not been artificially enlarged.
- Dashboard screenshots may include development indicators and fallback graphics. The page distinguishes these from recorded measurement data and does not reproduce screenshot values as validated numerical results.
- The separate handheld vibration measurement is experimental documentation, not a claimed integrated sensor in the energy-monitoring architecture.
